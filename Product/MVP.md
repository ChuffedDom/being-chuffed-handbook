An Minimal Viable Product is a term that is thrown around a lot. For Chuffed, the definition is:
>The quickest, easiest, and cheapest way to deliver value in completing a flow to validate a hypothesis.

## Rule of 2's

To help answer whenever your proposal is an MVP or not, you can use the rule of 2's to guide you.

- You can describe the functionality in **2 sentences.**
- You have no more than **2 features.**
- You can build it in **2 days.**
- It comes from **2 weeks research**.
- You have no more than **2 groups of target users**.
- You have **2 success metrics**.
- You have no more than **2 user stories**.
- You are proving no more than **2 concepts**.
