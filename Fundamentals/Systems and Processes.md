This page aims to guide Chuffed to create processes that work towards turning ideas into apps and not act as a barrier or bottleneck to creativity.

Chuffed aims to have Systems and Processes that follow these principles.
- Document, document, document
- Process is the enemy of Creativity
- The ultimate goal of a process is to make itself redundant
- Don't implement process for the sake of process
- Culture first; process after - Culture will eat process for breakfast
- Don't delay implementing the right process for the sake of managing the wrong process
